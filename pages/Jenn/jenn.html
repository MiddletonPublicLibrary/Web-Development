Today I'll be ranking my favorite snacks.

The List:

Gold Tier: Popcorn

This is my all time go-to snack. It's very convienient, and I have the cook time for my microwave down to a science. I do add extra butter and salt.

Silver Tier: Sunflower Seeds

This is my favorite snack when I just want something to nibble on but am not actually hungry. The downside is they can be messy because of the shells.

Bronze Tier: Chips and Salsa

I love chips and salsa! Also chips and guac, or nachos, or fritos and dip... basically, corn chips and a dip.

Conclusion:

I like salty and crunchy snacks the best!
